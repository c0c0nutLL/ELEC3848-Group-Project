Drive Pad

Hold a direction button to move. Release to stop.

Keyboard: `W A S D` or arrow keys for translation, `Q / E` for in-place turn, and Space for `STOP`.

Two-Servo Arm Control

Gripper Servo
90 deg
0 - 180 deg

Adjust the lift and gripper sliders, then send the selected angle to Arduino. Keyboard: `I / K` moves lift by 10 deg, `J / L` moves gripper by 10 deg. Short press steps once; hold repeats at 10 deg per second. Repeated short presses are merged into the latest target to reduce dropped commands and servo jitter.
